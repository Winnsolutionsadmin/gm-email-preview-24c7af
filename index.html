Before you come by, a little about who we are. Growing Mindfully is family owned and operated, founded in 2018, and the same family runs it day to day. We're a mindfulness-based school for infants through preschoolers, six minutes from the Capitol in downtown Sacramento.

Our teachers are the heart of the school. The educators who will greet you tomorrow have spent their careers doing this work: our leadership team alone brings 14 to 23 years each in early childhood education. People stay here because they love it, and children feel that every day.

Since you're touring with a five month old, you'll get to spend time in our infant room, where we strive for one educator for every three babies, tighter than California's 1:4 minimum. You'll also walk every room, infant through preschool, so you can picture the whole journey: children who start with us as babies grow up here through their last preschool year.
